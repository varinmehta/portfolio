## Overview

Development of an intuitive platform to help users organize, track, and discover books, inspired by Goodreads. ReadSync allows users to manage their personal libraries, track reading progress, and connect with a vibrant reading community.

## Key Features

-   **Book Tracking and Shelving**: Users can categorize books into custom shelves (e.g., "To Read," "Currently Reading," "Finished") and track their reading progress.
-   **Personalized Recommendations**: Leveraged user preferences and reading history to suggest books tailored to individual tastes.
-   **Social Features**: Enabled users to share reviews, rate books, and interact with friends by following their reading activities and recommendations.
-   **Search and Discover**: Advanced search functionality to explore books by title, author, or genre, and discover trending and top-rated books.
-   **Reading Stats**: Provided analytics for users to view their reading habits, such as the number of books read, average rating, and time spent reading.

## Technologies Used

-   **React.js**: For creating an interactive and responsive front-end interface.
-   **Node.js**: For building a scalable and efficient back-end with Express.
-   **MongoDB**: For managing user data, book records, and activity logs.
-   **Open Library API**: For fetching book metadata, including covers and descriptions.
-   **AWS**: Deployed the application using Elastic Beanstalk for scalability and reliability.

## Challenges and Learnings

One key challenge was managing the large volume of book data efficiently while ensuring fast response times. This was addressed by optimizing database queries and caching frequently accessed data. Additionally, balancing a clean user interface with feature-rich social interactions required iterative testing and feedback. Collaborative workflows between front-end and back-end teams ensured smooth integration and alignment with project goals.

## Outcome

ReadSync offers a seamless experience for book enthusiasts, simplifying book tracking and discovery. The platform fosters a community of readers while providing robust analytics for personal reading goals. The successful implementation highlights the team’s ability to develop scalable and user-friendly solutions for large-scale applications.
